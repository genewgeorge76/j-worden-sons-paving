Eastern Shore Poultry Plant

Heavy Industrial Paving & Infrastructure

The Challenge: Industrial poultry plants require surfaces capable of withstanding the extreme weight and high-frequency turning radius of fully loaded 18-wheeler tractor-trailers, 24 hours a day.

The Execution: We engineered and installed a heavy-duty, multi-course asphalt surface designed specifically for extreme industrial load-bearing capacity, ensuring long-term durability against heavy equipment and constant commercial traffic.

KFC & Taco Bell

Corporate New Builds & Remodels

The Challenge: Fast-food franchises require precise ADA compliance, efficient drive-thru lane grading, and minimal downtime during remodels to keep their business operational.

The Execution: Executed flawless paving for both complete ground-up new builds and rapid-turnaround remodels. We ensured exact grading for high-traffic drive-thru lanes, crisp line striping, and strict adherence to corporate blueprint specifications.

Walgreens & Rite Aid

High-Traffic Retail Parking

The Challenge: Retail pharmacies demand safe, accessible, and perfectly level parking surfaces for a diverse customer base, along with highly durable entryways for delivery trucks.

The Execution: Delivered comprehensive asphalt paving and striping solutions that prioritized pedestrian safety, seamless ADA access ramps, and heavy-duty delivery zones, maintaining the pristine aesthetic required by national retail brands.
