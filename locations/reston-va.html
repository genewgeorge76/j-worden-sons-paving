About This Area

Paving Reston Since 1984

As one of America's first modern planned communities, Reston's extensive network of roads and pathways is key to its design. We specialize in maintaining and resurfacing HOA roads and bike paths, as well as paving for the numerous tech companies that call Reston home.

J. Worden & Sons Asphalt Paving applies the same 6-inch compacted stone structural base standard on every project in Reston — the same engineering used in municipal roadways and high-traffic commercial sites. No shortcuts. No exceptions.
